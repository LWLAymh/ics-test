%%% stem
14. In a conventionally compiled ELF binary, which section normally holds writable global/static variables with nonzero initial values? **(2pts)**
%%% reference
答案：B，`.data`。这里明确限定非零初始化、可写的全局或静态变量；显式初始化为0的对象通常仍在 `.bss`，局部自动变量与只读常量也不能一概归入 `.data`。

`.symtab`
%%% option: B
`.data`
